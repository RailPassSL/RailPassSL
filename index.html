How It Works
From application to season ticket in just a few steps — fully digital, fully trackable.

Fill Online Form

Submit your application digitally with all required documents — no paper forms needed.

2

Institution Review

3

Station Approval

4

Collect Season

Visit the station with your reference number. Collect your season ticket instantly.
